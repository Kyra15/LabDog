# LabDog

This app allows users to understand their lab results, whether it be Complete Blood Count, Comprehensive Metabolic, or Thyroid Panels. We believe that the most important part of clinical screenings is the reaction. As such, LabDog allows users to plan how to improve their health based on these lab results by presenting a clinical interpretation of their results in straightforward language. Additionally, LabDog offers a chatbot service to help users to find doctors near them who specialize in their issues. With this, users can understand their current health and understand changes they can make to improve their health.
